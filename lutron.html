Lutron Light Switch
Automation

Enhance the technology and aesthetics of your living space with the Caseta Wireless Light Switch. Works with dimmable LEDs, CFLs, halogens, and incandescent bulbs.

Paired with the Lutron Smart Bridge and mobile app, you can control your lights remotely from anywhere. Integrates with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Ring. A Pico remote lets you control from anywhere in the home without rewiring.

Key features

Compatible with dimmable LED/CFL, halogen & incandescent
Remote control via Lutron App (Smart Bridge required)
Works with Alexa, Google Assistant & Ring
15-minute installation (neutral wire required)
Smart Away mode & automatic seasonal adjustments
App, voice & manual wall control
3-way setup via Pico remote — no rewiring
Handles up to 600W incandescent, 5A LED/CFL, 3A fans
